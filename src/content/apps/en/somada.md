Somada is for those who don’t mind spending time writing in their journal. For those who see the map as a canvas to fill with memories, photos, and adventures.

Every coffee place, monument, street, mountain peak, or remote lake is waiting to mean something special. The park where you had your first kiss, the road trip you took with someone you love, or the border you crossed while hiking. Somada is a place to save all those memories.

**Write and trace.** Each journal page lives where it happened, with its photos and a pin on the map. Draw the route stop by stop and choose how you travelled each leg: car, train, plane, boat, bike, on foot and more.

**Share it live.** Create a public link from any route. Whoever opens it sees the route and your pages in the browser, without installing anything, and new pages show up as you write them. You can stop sharing whenever you want.

**Relive it in a video.** Somada flies over your route and turns it into a video ready to post on social media.

On iPad and Mac, the map, the page and the route sit side by side, and the same journal follows you from one device to another.
