Somada is a travel journal for backpackers. It keeps the route, the days, and the photos together, instead of leaving the trip as a list of pins.

You trace the path on the map, attach pictures along it, and write the day where it happened. The same trip can be read as a globe, a paper map, or a journal.

I designed and built it on my own, and shipped it on the App Store in 2025. It runs on iPhone and iPad, in English and Spanish.
